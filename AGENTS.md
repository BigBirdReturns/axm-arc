# AXM Arc — contributor entry point

The canonical product ontology and execution priority live in `CLAUDE.md` under
**Product ontology and execution priority**. Read that section, then `ROADMAP.md`
and `STATUS.md`, before designing or changing anything. Do not restate the
ontology here; one source prevents doctrine drift. `HANDOFF.md` is historical
and is not a current queue.

Non-negotiable working rule: finish the reference game to a real product bar
without collapsing AXM into a game engine. When sequencing product experience
against platform proof, close the player loop first unless delay would harden an
irreversible schema, custody, determinism, save, or ledger defect.
