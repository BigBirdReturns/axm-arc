# Common Ship Pocket source format

**Authority:** *The Godscar Codex, Book III: The Common Ship*, foundational setting bible, first recension, July 2026.

`common-ship-pocket/1` is the creator-owned source plane for stories built from Book III. It compiles into an ordinary AXM Arc and is embedded unchanged under `Arc.extensions["godscar.common-ship@1"]`. The holder of the cartridge can therefore inspect, recover, fork, and redistribute the embodied operating grammar without a server or a second resolver.

Book III does not replace Books I or II. The evidence tiers, provenance discipline, faction receipts, control questions, no-clean-reset law, Crowning model, scarway latency, and maintained-misclassification mechanics remain binding. The Common Ship source adds **embodied operation** as a transversal field.

A Common Ship is not defined by hull size or visible species diversity. It is an inhabited vessel whose operating order treats incompatible embodiment as a permanent political fact and preserves more than one native user. A visibly diverse vessel can remain a host ship when every non-host body survives through private exceptions. A homogeneous crew can inhabit a common ship when its internal differences are recognized as constitutional rather than medical inconvenience.

## Nine required Watch Engine pressures

Every source declares these pressures in this exact order:

1. `vessel-form` — compact commonship, refugee ark, salvage ship, convoy tender, Counterborn body, Greatform organ, Dark Tomb courier, chronal vessel, mobile station, or another bounded moving polity;
2. `mission` — rescue, escort, audit, survey, migration, trade, repair, quarantine, first contact, return, escape, or another purpose that makes shared operation urgent;
3. `host-baseline` — the body, environment, clock, interface, or inherited architecture treated as the vessel's unmarked user;
4. `temporal-conflict` — the incompatible clocks, development, recovery, subjective resolution, or continuity spans shaping action;
5. `ordinary-good` — the school year, meal, promotion, wedding, child care, recovery, festival, family reunion, rest cycle, apprenticeship, private habitat, or other ordinary life worth preserving;
6. `excluded-actor` — the remote officer, untranslated child, sleeper, symbiont, habitat council, maintenance caste, ship fork, future generation, host organ, lineage-scarred population, or other actor omitted by the operating model;
7. `approaching-trigger` — scarway collapse, habitat failure, translator outage, reproductive threshold, emergency wake, external scan, succession, disease, Greatform phase change, sabotage evidence, or another event closing the decision horizon;
8. `cost-of-adaptation` — delay, life-fraction loss, exposure, loss of privacy, new dependency, habitat sacrifice, forced modification, depleted lineage store, command fragmentation, or increased compatibility debt;
9. `scale-revelation` — the fact that widens the map without making the immediate vessel irrelevant.

The nine selections must form one mechanism. The host baseline creates an adaptation tax. The mission makes that tax urgent. The excluded actor acquires force through the systems that translate, schedule, or omit it. The approaching trigger closes the decision horizon before every classification can become certain.

## Book I evidence discipline remains binding

The evidence ledger retains one of four tiers:

- `settled-canon`
- `contested-canon`
- `faction-doctrine`
- `story-facing-unknown`

It also records the claim, venue, legitimacy target, upside of acceptance, downside of acceptance, failure mode if false, and one or more receipts with source, intervention, and limits.

Book III adds one audit question to every claimed standard: **which body, clock, interface, and institution is native to that standard, and which bodies have been converted into exceptions?**

Local proof does not force universal consensus. Demonstrating that one ship can support several native users does not prove every baseline avoidable. Exposing one coercive translator does not prove mediation illegitimate. Showing that one emergency proxy exceeded authority does not establish that all delegated action is domination.

## Embodiment and lineage profiles

The source records bodies as operational facts rather than cosmetic species labels. `embodimentProfiles` names the scale, occupied volume, minimum passage, reach, locomotion, environmental envelope, sensorium, communication channels, interface paths, temporal structure, and lineage dependencies through which a person can participate. Cast members reference one profile through `profileId`, and each authored watch names the profiles whose presence or equivalent capacity the situation requires.

Profiles do not assign work. A large body is not automatically maintenance labor, a small body is not a vent-crawler, an aquatic person is not confined to care, and a fast or short-lived lineage is not permanent emergency staff. Roles state what the vessel needs; profiles state the conditions under which actual persons can perform, contest, or refuse that work.

Somatic scale is mechanically relevant source data. A profile can be micro-scale, small, human-scale, large, colossal, or distributed. The source records passage, volume, reach, manipulation scale, medium, pressure, temperature, gravity, radiation, and direct versus mediated interface paths so a later runtime can distinguish public access from a private workaround. The profile also carries the six Book III temporal dimensions in lineage-specific form, including explicit life-fraction accounting.

## Seven-system anatomy

A source carries the seven recurrent systems in order. These are operational jurisdictions rather than mandatory deck locations:

1. `transit-body` — hull, drive, scarway organs, radiators, docking structures, impact systems, navigation sensors, and material limits;
2. `habitat-bands` — atmospheres, solvents, pressure zones, gravity regimes, thermal ranges, radiation fields, substrates, symbionts, and private environments;
3. `common-thresholds` — locks, clinics, schools, courts, cargo interfaces, meeting chambers, and remote-presence paths where habitats exchange people, matter, information, and authority;
4. `translation-mesh` — persons, machines, protocols, environments, and legal routines converting meaning, tempo, sensorium, interface, environment, authority, and memory;
5. `watch-lattice` — roles, readiness, reserves, command succession, training, emergency proxies, and handoffs;
6. `continuity-commons` — medicine, nurseries, archives, lineage stores, food cultures, spare substrates, reproduction, education, funerary practice, and social institutions carrying life beyond one body or watch;
7. `sovereign-core` — mission purpose and the authority to redefine which systems remain common during crisis.

Each system records:

- its current use;
- the host assumption disappearing into its ordinary operation;
- the institution or population capable of revising that assumption.

## Six-dimensional temporal profile

A shared timestamp does not produce shared time. Every source declares:

1. `external-interval` — what ship clocks and operational standards count;
2. `subjective-resolution` — how much perception and thought occur within the interval;
3. `developmental-tempo` — how quickly competence, maturity, reproduction, succession, and decline occur;
4. `recovery-cycle` — sleep, torpor, feeding, maintenance, memory integration, and social synchronization;
5. `continuity-span` — how long a person, fork, lineage, or collective retains identity without succession or reconstruction;
6. `life-fraction-cost` — how much meaningful future, development, recovery, or continuity the interval consumes.

The dimensions are independent. No conversion such as one year equals several human years can substitute for an operational profile.

Each dimension records its operational use and the administrative-capture risk created when the institution knows enough to optimize it.

## Seven-layer translation stack

Words are only one boundary between bodies. The source carries these layers in order:

1. `meaning`
2. `tempo`
3. `sensorium`
4. `interface`
5. `environment`
6. `authority`
7. `memory-and-handoff`

Each layer records:

- the conversion being performed;
- the intermediary authoring the representation;
- the characteristic failure mode;
- the path through which the represented person may inspect, contest, or refuse it.

A fluent output is not presumed to be direct agency. Translation provenance must preserve source, transformation, loss, authorship, and contestability.

## Six Common Watch viability tests

A watch is the smallest unit through which the vessel becomes capable of action. It is a temporary polity, not merely a shift. Every source carries the six tests in order:

1. `role-coverage`
2. `temporal-overlap`
3. `habitat-compatibility`
4. `translation-resilience`
5. `handoff-continuity`
6. `life-fraction-fairness`

Each test records what it means, the condition for passing, and the consequence of failure. A roster may be qualified on paper while remaining impossible to inhabit.

## Eight ship-state tracks

For management-oriented play, each source records values from 0 through 4 for:

1. `habitat-integrity`
2. `temporal-coherence`
3. `translation-trust`
4. `roster-resilience`
5. `stores-and-care`
6. `continuity`
7. `visibility`
8. `compatibility-debt`

Most campaigns begin with the first seven tracks at 2 and Compatibility Debt at 1. A protective track at 0 enters crisis. Compatibility Debt at 4 means a person, patch, or private institution has become indispensable enough to govern the ship.

The values are narrative pressure, not physical measurement. A watch must change at least one track. Free repairs are rare because access, time, redundancy, and care consume real capacity.

## Incompatible cast responsibilities

A valid source contains at least one character responsible for each Book III function:

- `depends-on-host-baseline`
- `bears-adaptation-tax`
- `understands-maintenance-reality`
- `translates-excluded-actor`
- `benefits-from-delay`
- `sovereign-exception`

The role vocabulary follows the Common Watch diagram:

- `response`
- `mediation`
- `analysis`
- `maintenance`
- `care`
- `continuity`

Responsibility and role are related but not identical. A creator may assign any valid role to any responsibility when the local fiction supports it.

## Faction receipts

Every faction declares:

- the variable it attempts to control;
- the public good its method preserves;
- the characteristic failure produced by that same method.

Book III's operational constellation includes Final Humanity, the Quiet Directorate, the Manyborn, the Continuance, the Uncrowned Compact, Counterborn, and Great Ecological actors. The source format does not force a fixed faction list. It refuses moral-color factions without mechanisms and receipts.

A vessel may be Compact in charter, Directorate in scheduling, Final Humanity in medicine, Manyborn in habitat design, Continuance in archives, Counterborn in hull, and dependent on a Greatform route. Conflict begins when one subsystem claims that its variable has become the whole ship.

## Full operational ledger for every watch

Every authored watch records the Book III operational cycle before resolution:

### Declare the horizon

- when useful action closes;
- physical urgency;
- informational urgency;
- institutional urgency;
- manufactured urgency.

### Read the profiles

- required bodies;
- required habitats;
- required clocks;
- required translators;
- required reserves;
- life-fraction costs.

### Compose the watch

- absent actor;
- excluded body;
- dependency the arrangement cannot fully represent.

### Allocate common systems

- habitat bands;
- translation paths;
- direct interfaces;
- standby;
- stores;
- emergency authority.

### Conduct the handoff

- dissent;
- injury;
- readiness debt;
- promises;
- missing persons;
- uncertainty.

### Establish precedent

- what becomes newly possible;
- what becomes newly impossible;
- what becomes newly governable;
- what the next watch inherits as infrastructure.

This keeps the management problem attached to embodied people and prevents a successful roll from producing a clean reset.

## Four compiled movements

The compiler maps authored watches into four ordinary Arc progression tiers:

1. `ordinary-life` — establish the school, meal, family, recovery cycle, private habitat, or other good operation is meant to preserve;
2. `compose-watch` — assemble roles, bodies, clocks, habitats, translators, reserves, and named absences;
3. `resolve-pressure` — act before classification becomes certain and alter the ship state through a concrete allocation;
4. `handoff` — preserve dissent, injury, debt, promises, missing persons, uncertainty, and constitutional precedent.

The compiled Arc uses six attributes:

- Care
- Systems
- Translation
- Continuity
- Judgment
- Resolve

It uses the six Common Watch roles, exact named founding cast, authored access milestones, a bounded opening decision, and an `arc_complete` event naming the inherited consequences.

## Mission Physics

All ten Book III invariants are required and true:

- no neutral environment;
- a clock is not an experience;
- translation has authorship;
- a roster is an ecology;
- every accommodation creates dependency;
- emergency reveals the native user;
- handoffs are political events;
- travel spends unequal life;
- the vessel may be a person;
- every mission revises the constitution.

A source that disables one is not a valid `common-ship-pocket/1` object.

## Consequences

Every watch points to a persistent consequence. Available classes include Book I's citizens, dependencies, routes, archives, doctrines, adaptive capacities, and trauma, plus Book III's habitats, schedules, interfaces, readiness debt, recovery debt, compatibility debt, continuity, visibility, and jurisdiction.

The ending resolves one decision while making something:

- newly possible;
- newly impossible;
- newly governable;
- inherited as infrastructure by a future body or watch.

## Compilation and custody

`compileCommonShipPocket()` produces an engine `1.2.0` Arc with:

- domain `godscar-common-ship`;
- exact creator identity and named cast;
- the four compiled movements;
- milestone-gated watches;
- an opening choice between marking the baseline and accepting a bounded emergency baseline;
- persistent completion rewards naming the changed constitution;
- the exact source under `godscar.common-ship@1`.

No executable plugin code is accepted. The extension remains JSON-safe, namespaced, and versioned.

`readCommonShipPocketExtension()` recovers and revalidates the embedded source.

## Authoring seed

`src/common-ship/templates.ts` exports `COMMON_SHIP_STARTER`, a structurally complete **private-branch** seed. It is not a canonical vessel or the first Book III campaign. Its purpose is to demonstrate every required field and provide a deterministic compiler fixture without prematurely deciding *The Common Watch*, *The Relief Circuit*, *The First Accord*, or any other reference cartridge.

## Current boundary

This source plane encodes the Codex into executable law. It does **not** yet claim:

- a canonical Common Ship reference campaign;
- deterministic runtime evaluation of profile compatibility and watch viability;
- a dedicated guided Watch Forge UI;
- a World Common Ship receiver or vessel-management plane;
- a new bundled World cartridge;
- production ship, habitat, crew, interface, convoy, or Greatform assets;
- completion of the First Charter Hall asset lane, Waking Tower generalization, or Ilyon generalization.

Book III is subsequent source authority. The current World release remains the accepted three-cartridge boundary: The First Charter, The Waking Tower, and The Kind Gods of Ilyon. Dark Tomb and Common Ship source planes must not be inferred into that release until separate runtime, asset, and browser acceptance trains exist.
