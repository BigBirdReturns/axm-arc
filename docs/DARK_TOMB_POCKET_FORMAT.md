# Dark Tomb Pocket source format

**Authority:** *The Godscar Codex, Book II: The Dark Tomb*, foundational canon, first recension, July 2026.

`dark-tomb-pocket/1` is the creator-owned source plane for stories built from Book II. It compiles into an ordinary AXM Arc and is embedded unchanged under `Arc.extensions["godscar.dark-tomb@1"]`. The holder of the cartridge can therefore inspect, recover, fork, and redistribute the Tomb grammar without a server or a second resolver.

Book II does not replace Book I. The four canon/evidence tiers, provenance discipline, faction receipts, control questions, incompatible responsibilities, and no-clean-reset law remain binding. The Dark Tomb source adds the civilizational form of **maintained misclassification**.

A Dark Tomb is not defined by geology. It is an inhabited place or distributed system whose survival depends on another actor continuing to classify it as dead, primitive, ruined, empty, harmless, quarantined, or already consumed.

## Eight required pressures

Every source declares these pressures in this exact order:

1. **tomb form** — crustal city, ruined outpost, dead fleet, desolate scarway edge, Potemkin world, living Greatform, distributed system, chronal pocket, or another bounded inhabited absence;
2. **exterior lie** — the classification presented beyond the protected map;
3. **custodian** — the office, guild, court, machine, ecology, lineage, host, or coalition that sustains the lie;
4. **ordinary good** — the clinic, school, wedding, market, food culture, park, family route, festival, repair dock, election, or other life that makes concealment worth preserving;
5. **excluded actor** — the population, ecology, machine fork, illegal child, sealed district, archived witness, host organ, refugee, or other actor omitted by the current map;
6. **approaching breach** — the scan, radiator failure, scarway opening, signal, collapse, recovered archive, awakening guardian, audit, or other event making classification urgent;
7. **cost of opening or closing** — exposure, disease, famine, erased population, lost autonomy, permanent isolation, incorporated refuge, inherited war, or another concrete cost;
8. **scale revelation** — the fact that widens the map without making the immediate hub irrelevant.

The eight selections must form one mechanism. The custodian's method sustains the exterior lie. The lie distributes risk. The excluded actor acquires force through the systems that omit it. The breach makes the classification decision irreversible.

## Book I evidence discipline remains binding

The evidence ledger retains one of four tiers:

- `settled-canon`
- `contested-canon`
- `faction-doctrine`
- `story-facing-unknown`

It also records:

- the claim;
- the venue capable of certifying it;
- the legitimacy target;
- the upside of accepting it;
- the downside of accepting it;
- the failure mode if it is false;
- one or more receipts, each with source, intervention, and limits.

Book II adds one audit question to every claim of emptiness, primitiveness, ruin, or death: **which institution produced that classification, and which actors did it exclude?**

Local proof does not force universal consensus. Proving one Alarm obsolete does not prove every Alarm fraudulent. Exposing one predatory custodian does not prove opening safe. Discovering one inhabited ruin does not establish that every ruin contains a society.

## Seven-layer anatomy

A source carries the seven recurrent layers in order. They are political and functional layers, not mandatory geological floors:

1. `grave-skin` — the profile that absorbs inspection and supplies the first explanation;
2. `shroud` — the Potemkin, technical, ecological, and informational system sustaining the explanation;
3. `quiet-works` — heat, waste, relay, reservoir, camouflage, and maintenance systems that make absence physically plausible;
4. `common-depths` — homes, schools, markets, farms, clinics, workshops, kitchens, art, worship, and public life;
5. `custodial-ring` — maps, sensors, routes, archives, credentials, and emergency authority;
6. `war-layer` — original defenses, commands, weapons, enemy remains, sealed populations, and inherited target logic;
7. `black-core` — the fact, actor, archive, or system the present order cannot safely reveal, destroy, or admit it no longer understands.

Each layer records:

- its current use;
- its inherited purpose;
- its official classification.

A room is therefore never treated as empty merely because no visible person stands inside it. Systems, ecologies, atmosphere, memories, structural stress, and legal claims continue to act.

## Five-dimensional depth vector

Physical distance is only one coordinate. Every Tomb source declares:

1. `material` depth — meters, hulls, crust, pressure, locks, distance, and environmental barriers;
2. `signal` depth — transformations between activity and the wake that reaches an observer;
3. `administrative` depth — permissions, jurisdictions, credentials, taboos, and social boundaries;
4. `historical` depth — collapsed orders, forgotten purposes, inherited wars, and displaced claims;
5. `interpretive` depth — false histories, staged surfaces, taxonomies, assumptions, and myths that cause a present fact to be misclassified.

Each delve assigns integer coordinates from 0 through 5 to the relevant dimensions. The vector is not a universal rank. A surface village can be more deeply hidden than a buried vault when the village is the better lie.

## Signature budget

Every inhabited system produces wake. The source names:

- the observer or sensor regime;
- the exterior classification that must remain credible;
- the important wake sources;
- the operations used to manage them;
- the ordinary-life allocations competing for detectable capacity.

The six available operations are:

- `sink` — store heat, waste, traffic, or information until a safer interval;
- `spread` — distribute wake across large areas or long periods;
- `shift` — move wake to another site, season, orbit, organism, route, or decoy;
- `mask` — embed activity within plausible geology, weather, stellar noise, or host metabolism;
- `counterfeit` — present the wake as another source that completes the exterior account;
- `sacrifice` — permit one visible layer or population to absorb discovery so deeper layers remain hidden.

A particular tomb may use any non-empty subset. Every operation records its cost. The signature budget governs detectability, not morality: low-wake action can be cruel, and high-wake action can be necessary for survival or freedom.

## The Long Alarm

The source records:

- the original threat;
- the audit problem that prevents later generations from safely establishing whether it has ended;
- the current Alarm phase;
- the six common phases in order.

The phases are:

1. `shadow` — detect possible attention or unexplained wake;
2. `hush` — suspend nonessential activity and reduce the signature budget;
3. `fold` — move vulnerable populations and functions behind deeper barriers;
4. `black` — shut down routes, surfaces, relays, and archives that connect layers;
5. `cut` — sever systems, routes, memories, or populations judged capable of transmitting the whole structure;
6. `wake` — restore activity and decide which emergency restrictions survive.

Each phase records both the protection it supplies and the internal cost it imposes. The Long Alarm is an audit problem and a political order, not merely a mood.

## Incompatible cast responsibilities

A valid source contains at least one character responsible for each Book II function:

- `depends-on-alarm`
- `bears-cost-of-concealment`
- `understands-quiet-works`
- `translates-excluded-actor`
- `holds-map-changing-evidence`
- `benefits-from-delay`
- `sovereign-exception`

The role vocabulary is:

- `wakekeeper`
- `surface-bearer`
- `maintainer`
- `interlocutor`
- `witness`
- `deliberator`
- `exception`

Responsibility and role are related but not identical. A creator may assign any valid role to any responsibility when the local fiction supports it.

## Faction receipts

Every faction declares:

- the variable it attempts to control;
- the public good its method preserves;
- the characteristic failure produced by that same method.

Book II's underworld methods include acceleration, permanent administration, proliferation, preservation of strata, constitutional opacity, created guardianship, and shelter or incorporation by a living host. The source format does not force a fixed faction list. It does refuse moral-color factions without receipts.

## Expedition ledger

Every delve records the six facts Book II requires before departure:

1. objective;
2. authorized route;
3. signature budget;
4. institution granting authority;
5. claim that must be proven;
6. inheritance likely to remain after return.

This keeps the dungeon crawl attached to the hub and prevents rooms from becoming consequence-free content islands.

## Four compiled movements

The compiler maps authored delves into four ordinary Arc progression tiers:

1. `ordinary-life` — establish the hub, the ordinary good, and the dependency;
2. `descent` — cross inherited purpose, jurisdiction, and buried systems;
3. `breach` — act before the new classification can be made certain;
4. `return` — record the changed route, population, dependency, visibility, Alarm, or claim to control.

The compiled Arc uses six attributes:

- Care
- Evidence
- Systems
- Jurisdiction
- Opacity
- Resolve

It uses the seven roles above, exact named founding cast, authored access milestones, a bounded opening decision, and an `arc_complete` event naming the inherited consequences.

## Story Physics

All ten Book II invariants are required and true:

- no perfect invisibility;
- every comfort has wake;
- every layer has residue;
- a map is a political claim;
- defenses outlive enemies;
- external opacity can Crown within;
- the hub is the story;
- every delve changes the tomb;
- treasure creates constituencies;
- scale is distributed.

A source that disables one is not a valid `dark-tomb-pocket/1` object.

## Consequences

Every delve points to a persistent consequence. Available consequence classes include Book I's citizens, dependencies, routes, archives, doctrines, adaptive capacities, and trauma, plus Book II's visibility, jurisdiction, Alarm state, habitat, and constituency changes.

The ending resolves one decision while altering what is:

- newly possible;
- newly impossible;
- newly visible;
- newly hidden;
- newly subject to control.

## Compilation and custody

`compileDarkTombPocket()` produces an engine `1.3.0` Arc with:

- domain `godscar-dark-tomb`;
- exact creator identity and named cast;
- the four compiled movements;
- milestone-gated delves;
- Long Alarm, signature credibility, and external visibility as engine-owned state;
- one exact boolean state for every authored inherited consequence;
- success-state effects that write the changed Alarm, signature, visibility, and consequence record with before-and-after receipts;
- an opening choice between preserving the Alarm and authorizing a bounded breach;
- persistent completion rewards naming the changed map;
- the exact source under `godscar.dark-tomb@1`.

No executable plugin code is accepted. The extension remains JSON-safe, namespaced, and versioned.

`readDarkTombPocketExtension()` recovers and revalidates the embedded source.

## Private authoring seed

`src/dark-tomb/templates.ts` exports `DARK_TOMB_STARTER`, a structurally complete **private-branch** seed. It demonstrates every required field and remains available as the neutral starting point for a new Tomb.

## Canonical reference: The Lamp District

`src/dark-tomb/lamp-district.ts` is the canonical Book II reference source. It compiles into the bundled `LAMP_DISTRICT` Arc and the portable artifacts:

- `cartridges/lamp-district.tomb.json`;
- `cartridges/lamp-district.arc.json`.

The eight authored movements carry the player from school-lamp rationing and reservoir authority through the drainage liturgy, sleeping market, dormant war lattice, surface-sacrifice policy, clinic heat return, and a public redrawing of the district map. A completed run leaves the Alarm awake, the signature breached, the Tomb exposed, and all eight civic consequences recorded in engine-owned state.

Rebuild the artifacts with `npm run build:dark-tomb-reference`. Source, compiled Arc, artifacts, full-campaign reachability, exact named cast, and final state inheritance are permanent tests.

## Dark Tomb Forge

The browser product exposes a dedicated Dark Tomb Forge with guided and exact-source modes over the same `dark-tomb-pocket/1` object. It can:

- start from the private Book II seed;
- load The Lamp District;
- edit identity, pressures, evidence, Alarm, signature budget, layers, depth, cast, consequences, delves, expedition ledgers, checks, and Story Physics;
- preserve a local exact draft;
- validate and compile through the canonical source-plane registry;
- run bounded deterministic campaign sweeps;
- install the compiled Arc;
- export source and compiled artifacts;
- open the cartridge in the ordinary player.

## Current boundary

Gate 3 completes the Arc-side canonical Dark Tomb reference and authoring product. It does **not** yet claim:

- a dedicated World Underworld receiver or visual plane;
- the Lamp District spatial hub, layered map, and browser journey;
- final production art, map, wake, Alarm, or resident presentation;
- *The Bird Over Kesh* acceptance.

Those are later product trains built from this source and engine authority rather than invented inside the receiver.
