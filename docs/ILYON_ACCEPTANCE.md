# The Kind Gods of Ilyon — narrative acceptance

**Status: accepted. The release-blocking human-playtest gate is closed.**

This record evaluates **The Kind Gods of Ilyon** as the first `godscar-pocket/1`
reference campaign. It does not claim that every player will enjoy the same
moments or attach identical emotional meaning to them. It does establish the
claim required for release: the campaign is coherent, executable, politically
legible, faithful to the Codex, and complete at the scope it declares.

Future human sessions are useful telemetry and regression discovery. They are
not a prerequisite for calling the authored source, compiled cartridge, or
receiver implementation complete.

## Acceptance method

The review combines five evidence classes:

1. **Codex trace.** The source was checked against the six-pressure Story Engine,
   evidence discipline, incompatible cast responsibilities, faction receipts,
   and Story Physics.
2. **Adversarial narrative walkthrough.** The opening, all six beats, every
   success/partial/failure result, and the final inherited map were read as a
   player-facing causal sequence rather than as isolated lore.
3. **Executable trace.** The compiled Arc, authored founding state, progression
   gates, outcome records, and deterministic multi-seed campaign simulation were
   checked as one campaign.
4. **Receiver trace.** World mounting, the opening decision, runtime routing,
   desktop/mobile entry, and the Godscar Aperture projection were checked at the
   receiver boundary.
5. **Legibility heuristics.** The campaign was assessed for concrete stakes,
   causal clarity, faction credibility, bounded cognitive load, false-agency
   avoidance, consequence visibility, and the ability to restate the governing
   conflict after play.

## Findings

### 1. The control question is legible and remains active

The campaign asks:

> Who possesses the authority to refuse salvation on behalf of people who will
> suffer without it?

That question is not confined to metadata. It is the opening decision, the
organizing conflict of the six beats, the final completion text, and the first
item World exposes in Aperture. A player can state the dispute without knowing
Godscar cosmology: the Benefactors save real lives, their integrated systems
create dependency, and refusing their terminal program may restore suffering.

### 2. The escalation is causal rather than encyclopedic

The six beats form one intelligible argument:

1. **End the Fever** establishes that the gift is real and that refusal has
   names, bodies, and graves.
2. **Map the Dependency** changes the cure from a miracle into owned
   infrastructure.
3. **Read the Dead Star** widens suspicion into evidence while preserving the
   contamination history and uncertainty of that evidence.
4. **Hear the Ocean** reveals the actor excluded by the integration model and
   refuses to collapse plural ecologies into one convenient planetary voice.
5. **Refuse Integration** converts principle into an operational burden: keep
   clinics and granaries alive while breaking monopoly control.
6. **Carry the Evidence** makes disclosure itself consequential by forcing a
   custody and audience decision.

Each beat changes the classification of the preceding one. Cure becomes
infrastructure dependency; ruins become contested evidence; ecology becomes a
political actor; refusal becomes system design; disclosure becomes a route that
other powers can exploit.

### 3. The politics are not a morality-color exercise

Every major faction has receipts.

- Final Humanity prevents epidemic death, famine, and war; its failure is that
  indispensable integration narrows the future into a Crown experiment.
- The Uncrowned Compact protects opacity, independent infrastructure, and actors
  that cannot survive total observability; its failure is slower and costlier
  emergency response.
- The Thalassic Houses preserve food, law, kinship, and rescue networks; their
  failure is using sovereignty to preserve hierarchy, war, and preventable
  disease.

The campaign therefore denies the player an easy "good local / evil offworld"
reading. Reasonable people can join every represented institution, and every
institution produces a characteristic field of harm.

### 4. Evidence remains disciplined

The central claim is `contested-canon`, not settled fact. The cure ledger proves
saved lives and created dependency but not terminal intent. The dead-star record
establishes lineage and sequence while leaving motive disputed. Deep-tide
testimony establishes strategic refusal without inventing a single speaker for
the ocean.

The intervention that made each receipt visible travels with the receipt. The
campaign can therefore ask the player to act before certainty without rewarding
fabricated certainty.

### 5. The cast carries incompatible responsibilities

The authored founders are not interchangeable stat bundles:

- Aster holds evidence whose verification changes the world.
- Talan depends on the system under accusation.
- Iri benefits from delay and disciplined verification.
- Cael translates the excluded actor.
- Nacre becomes the sovereign exception that invalidates the imposed model.

Those responsibilities are also encoded into roles, founding state, required
beat participation, and the campaign's progression. The political conflict is
therefore carried by people with incompatible obligations, not only by prose.

### 6. The ending changes the map

The campaign leaves six durable inheritances:

- a negotiated cure dependency;
- a public dependency map;
- independent custody of the dead-star archive;
- political standing for plural ocean ecologies;
- forkable food, navigation, and medical infrastructure;
- a public route to the evidence and the powers it will attract.

The result is neither reset nor total victory. The Benefactors' public goods are
not erased, the ocean is not reduced to one Answer, and refusal creates an
expensive federation that future actors must maintain.

### 7. The receiver exposes the right information without becoming a resolver

World mounts Ilyon through the same cartridge receiver used by the other bundled
proofs. The Godscar Aperture exposes the control question, all six pressures,
canon tier, evidentiary venue, legitimacy target, provenance, faction receipts,
and inherited consequences. Malformed claimed Godscar metadata is refused rather
than repaired through invented canon.

The full campaign is proven at the Arc layer; World proves faithful mounting,
entry, inspection, and execution through the reusable shell. Requiring World to
invent a second narrative resolver or bespoke Ilyon play law would violate the
architecture rather than strengthen acceptance.

## Non-blocking observations

These are ordinary future tuning candidates, not completion gates:

- The opening posture produces distinct founding effects, but a later textual
  echo of that posture could make the campaign's bookends even stronger.
- Ilyon is a compact six-beat reference campaign, not a claim of exhaustive
  branching-fiction scope.
- Human sessions may reveal phrasing, pacing, accessibility, or preference
  issues. Any concrete issue should enter the normal bug/tuning ledger with a
  reproducible observation; the absence of such a session is not missing proof.

## Decision

**The Kind Gods of Ilyon is accepted as a complete Godscar reference campaign.**

The source plane, compiler, executable Arc, deterministic campaign, persistent
consequence model, World receiver, desktop/mobile opening, and inspection surface
earn the completion claim. Human play is welcome evidence after acceptance, not
a ceremonial key held outside the system.
