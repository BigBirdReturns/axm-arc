# Human blind trial protocol

## Purpose

The executable estate proves structural nativehood. It does not prove that the resulting stories are emotionally effective, funny where intended, memorable, or equal to work produced by the original room. This protocol creates the next evidence tier without allowing qualification receipts or authorship labels to contaminate the review.

## Materials

The reviewer receives `receipts/human-review-packet.json`. The evaluator retains `receipts/human-review-answer-key.json`. The packet contains opaque story IDs, one public universe primer per story, a mechanism statement, a semantic beat outline, and a common scoring form. It omits pitch titles, source object IDs, compiler outcomes, and authorship labels.

A full comparative trial should add an equal number of original-room or accepted-reference cases rendered through the same review schema. Their source labels remain in a separate evaluator key.

## Reviewer task

Reviewers score each case from one through seven on universe nativehood, character causality, evidence discipline, earned consequence, surprise without arbitrariness, emotional potential, and desire to continue. They also identify the strongest element, weakest element, any contradiction or drift, and the minimum revision required before acceptance.

The authorship guess is recorded as `original`, `successor`, or `uncertain`. Reviewers are told that formatting is normalized and must not be used as an authorship cue.

## Recommended seats

Use at least four distinct seats:

1. a continuity reader familiar with the universe;
2. a cold genre reader unfamiliar with the production history;
3. a character-focused editor;
4. a systems or game-narrative designer.

No reviewer should have participated in creating the candidate pitch being scored.

## Decision thresholds

A successor mechanism is provisionally native when its median universe-nativehood, character-causality, evidence-discipline, and earned-consequence scores are each at least five, no required dimension receives a median below four, and fewer than one quarter of reviewers identify a concrete continuity contradiction.

A quality-equivalence claim requires a mixed reference-and-successor trial. Compare distributions rather than only averages. The successor set should not be inferior on nativehood or character causality, and its lower quartile on desire to continue should remain within one scale point of the reference set. Authorship classification substantially above chance is evidence that the rooms remain distinguishable and should trigger qualitative diagnosis rather than automatic rejection.

A quality-superiority claim requires the successor set to retain the equivalence floor while exceeding the reference distribution on at least one predeclared dimension, such as surprise, emotional potential, or desire to continue. The dimension must be declared before revealing the answer key.

## Failure handling

Low nativehood with high surface familiarity indicates noun imitation. Low character causality indicates actor puppeteering. Low evidence discipline indicates unjustified certainty. Low earned consequence indicates a detachable episode. High nativehood with low desire to continue indicates that the rails preserved identity while failing as drama. Each diagnosis routes to a different authoring correction.

## Control question

Can reviewers who do not know which room produced a case recognize the same universe mechanism and character causality while still finding the successor material surprising, emotionally live, and worth continuing?
