# Deterministic narrative succession and cold-room qualification

**Status:** additive post-v1 prototype  
**Authority family:** `src/narrative/succession/`  
**Qualified handoff sources:** The Kind Gods of Ilyon and The Lamp District  
**Runtime requirement:** deterministic local execution; no model, service, account, or network call at play time

## Decision

AXM Arc treats continuing-universe authorship as a constrained selection and memory problem. The original creator authors a finite constitutional source plane. A successor room authors mechanism pitches and expression. The runtime compiles the source into a plot-excluded handoff packet, binds actors to current goals and available knowledge, rejects proposals that violate identity or causality, selects among the remaining alternatives through a deterministic disclosed score, commits exact beats and obligations, and audits whether later story state actually depends on what happened.

The system does not attempt to synthesize prose as authority. Presentation remains downstream. No dialogue, joke, staging choice, or generated line may add a fact, intention, choice, effect, or consequence absent from the semantic receipt.

## The finite original-creator obligation

The original creator supplies the universe constitution rather than an episode backlog. The source plane must name:

1. the controlling question;
2. recurrent pressures;
3. the public good preserved by each institution;
4. the characteristic failure produced by the same institutional method;
5. actors carrying incompatible responsibilities;
6. characteristic methods by which those actors pursue goals;
7. forbidden methods or deviations that require evidence;
8. evidence claims, venues, interventions, provenance, and limits;
9. concrete costs that prevent clean answers;
10. persistent consequence classes;
11. Story Physics that cannot be waived;
12. positive, negative, and boundary cases used to calibrate acceptance.

That authority is versioned and content-addressed. The creator's continuing work is constitutional change control and calibration. It is not daily supervision of every scene.

## The successor-room input

A successor writer authors a bounded mechanism pitch:

```text
ordinary good
characteristic actor method
active pressure
affected actor or constituency
evidence limit
concrete cost
persistent change
control question
source references
```

The pitch does not contain an episode graph. The compiler binds local actors, goals, beliefs, evidence, consequences, and the applicable incident family. It then emits the smallest causal rail capable of making the conflict authoritative.

## Authority chain

```text
creator-owned source plane
  -> source completeness validation
  -> plot-excluded handoff packet
  -> typed incident portfolio and successor pitches
  -> deterministic actor and role binding
  -> character intention and knowledge qualification
  -> identity and Story Physics gates
  -> family-specific causal micro-rail
  -> eligible candidate set
  -> bounded deterministic selection
  -> beat, obligation, and consequence ledger
  -> counterfactual causal audit
  -> presentation
```

Invalid candidates are rejected before comparative ranking. Authored priority cannot rescue a missing intention, unavailable knowledge, forbidden character method, missing identity anchor, illegal rail transition, absent causal parent, or authoritative beat that changes no persistent state.

## Character agency

Every authoritative actor move cites:

- the acting character;
- the characteristic method used;
- one or more active goals owned by that character;
- propositions the character knows, believes, or is explicitly permitted to suspect;
- expected effects on the cited goals;
- disclosed risk.

The world truth and the actor's belief may differ. A false belief remains a valid motive when it has provenance and sufficient confidence. The receipt identifies the false proposition. This permits misunderstanding, deception, failed plans, and dramatic irony without giving a character access to author knowledge.

A move fails when it uses another actor's goal, cites an inactive goal, relies on unknown information, contradicts a proposition the actor explicitly disbelieves, serves none of the cited intentions, or conceals its expected risk.

## Micro-rail correction

The first complete prototype produced a false success. Every episode passed identity, agency, obligation, and consequence checks, but all incident families used the same six-function rail. The series varied its nouns while repeating one structural formula.

Continuing episodes now compile through three family-specific causal grammars:

| Incident family | Required micro-rail |
|---|---|
| Faction overreach | `establish -> pressure -> escalate -> choose -> consequence` |
| Evidence limit | `establish -> reveal -> pressure -> choose -> consequence` |
| Consequence claim | `establish -> pressure -> choose -> consequence` |

The founding campaign retains the complete six-function rail because it must establish the universe mechanism. Series incidents use only the functions required by their causal family. Every accepted rail still terminates in a persistent consequence.

## Story memory

The authoritative memory unit is a connected receipt rather than a prose recap. Each committed beat records:

```text
source incident and family
semantic function
actors and characteristic methods
intentions
knowledge and beliefs
required state payments
state payments produced
causal parents
obligations opened
obligations resolved
eligible alternatives
selection regret
```

Promises, grievances, legitimacy claims, dependencies, unresolved evidence, exposure risks, and constituency claims persist as obligations. A later beat resolving an obligation cites its opening beat. A story thread is therefore reconstructed from connected receipts rather than manually maintained as a parallel season outline.

The series scheduler supports one primary obligation, a secondary obligation, deferral, collision, closure, and bounded inheritance into the next season. The tested seasons retain one or two inherited obligations and keep obligation age at two episodes or less.

## Deterministic plurality

Selection is deterministic without being singular. Candidates first pass hard qualification. The selector then scores identity coverage, source severity, obligation pressure, actor diversity, responsibility fit, local-cast relevance, intention strength, freshness, and repetition. Several qualified candidates may enter a bounded score band. Seeded selection operates only inside that band, and the receipt records the regret accepted relative to the highest score.

The same packet, state, seed, and choices reproduce the same result. Different seeds can select different already-qualified mechanisms, casts, methods, and presentation orders. Novelty therefore operates after native identity and causal validity have been proven.

## Writer-room operating surface

The qualified semantic estate compiles into `axm-narrative-writer-room-packet/1`. Each episode brief contains the incident mechanism, controlling question, continuity debt, semantic and presentation order, actors and characteristic methods, intention and knowledge receipts, persistent payments, and a short room-question set. The packet is an assignment and rewrite surface. It does not reopen facts, goals, consequences, or causal law to improvisation.

The representative build emits twelve briefs per universe with zero creator exceptions, all three incident families, four presentation forms, and successor-authored mechanisms in the ordinary season mix. The room may improve dialogue, staging, humor, pacing, and emotional expression while the semantic receipt remains the authority boundary.

## Physical cold-room boundary

The strict worker receives only:

```text
agency.js
cold-room.js
determinism.js
model.js
series.js
source-validation.js
ilyon-handoff.json
lamp-district-handoff.json
```

The worker contains no source fixture, source compiler, reference beat, reference delve, canonical campaign text, or source-specific branch. Runtime files are scanned for source IDs, titles, actor IDs, and actor names. Any source-specific token is a qualification failure.

## Current qualification

The current exact receipt is `tests/narrative/succession/receipts/isolated-blind-room-report.json`.

For each handoff packet, the isolated worker completed:

- one deterministic six-beat founding walk;
- one representative twelve-episode series;
- 1,000 twelve-episode seasons;
- 256 fifty-episode seasons.

Across both universes this is 2,000 twelve-episode seasons and 512 fifty-episode seasons inside the physical isolation gate. The twelve-episode matrices produced 1,000 distinct incident sequences and 1,000 distinct cast-and-method sequences per universe. All tested seasons retained all three incident families, zero failed episodes, zero counterfactually loose beats, no identical adjacent skeleton run, bounded actor concentration, and an obligation age of two episodes or less.

The fifty-episode runs exercised all twelve available Ilyon incidents and all fifteen available Lamp incidents in every tested season. All 512 long-horizon seasons passed.

A 31-case mutation matrix also corrupts plot exclusion, identity anchors, actor methods, goal ownership, source references, causal order, belief confidence, fingerprints, and required source estates. Every mutation is rejected with the expected diagnostic code.

## Plot-excluded successor authorship

The acceptance estate includes successor mechanisms authored from the handoff packets rather than the reference plots:

- **The Diagnostic Fork Hearing**, concerning custody of an indispensable medical diagnostic standard;
- **The Funeral Heat Hearing**, concerning whether mourning may be classified as discretionary wake;
- **The Reef Passage Hearing**, concerning whether a necessary shipping corridor can also be another polity's body;
- **The Night Market Heat Audit**, concerning whether public life can be suppressed by moving the same heat into households excluded from the official model.

Each pitch compiles into a native incident, produces an agency-qualified micro-rail, enters at least one deterministic season, and retains its pitch identity in every beat. Cross-universe transplantation is rejected by source-reference authority rather than by a similarity heuristic.

## What is proven

The executable evidence proves that a substantially new team can be constrained to produce structurally native material without possessing the original plot or the original room's tacit memory. It proves deterministic identity gates, character agency, epistemic limits, causal ordering, persistent obligations, consequence inheritance, bounded plurality, and source-specific transplantation refusal.

It also proves that several plausible false successes are detectable: surface imitation without mechanism, plot movement without character intention, unresolved obligations masquerading as closure, seed variation without actual plurality, incident-name variety with identical cast skeletons, family coverage starvation, and a universal episode formula hidden beneath varied content.

## What remains unproven

The system does not yet prove audience-perceived equality or superiority. Structural nativehood can coexist with dull dialogue, weak humor, low emotional intensity, poor pacing, or scenes that satisfy the ledger while failing as drama. The next evidence tier is a human cold-team trial followed by blind reader or player comparison against reference material.

The venue must separate continuity judgment from preference. Reviewers should score character recognition, causal legibility, emotional payoff, surprise, humor where intended, desire to continue, and whether the story appears to have been produced by the original room. The successor team must receive only the handoff packet, pitch form, qualification tools, and a bounded expression guide.

## Evidence ledger

The evidence tier is executable structural qualification. The venue is the deterministic TypeScript estate, physical six-module isolation boundary, plot-excluded handoff packets, adversarial rejection cases, 2,000 twelve-episode seasons, 512 fifty-episode seasons, and successor-pitch trials. The target is the claim that continuing-universe mechanism, character agency, causal inheritance, and production handoff can be made explicit enough for a mostly new team to operate. The upside is durable authorship without permanent dependence on one room. The downside is overformalization. The principal failure mode is structurally compliant material that is aesthetically inert.

## Control question

Can a writer who never sat in the original room prove, from the receipts alone, which world pressure produced the story, why these actors used these methods from the goals and beliefs actually available to them, which prior beat the story inherited, what persistent state changed, which alternatives were rejected, and what future decision became unavoidable?
