# The Relief Circuit acceptance contract

**Status:** Gate 5 preparation. This document freezes the target and dependency order; it does not claim the canonical cartridge, Common Ship Forge, connected Lamp District run, or World receiver have shipped.

**Authority:** *The Godscar Codex, Book III: The Common Ship*, first recension; `common-ship-pocket/1`; engine 1.3 state and composition law; and the frozen RODOH 1.0 acceptance contract.

The Relief Circuit is accepted only when one creator-owned Common Ship source can be edited, validated, compiled, completed, inspected, exported, recovered, installed in World, connected to The Lamp District, and resumed without World inventing watch feasibility, ship-state effects, handoff consequences, or constitutional precedent.

## Canonical custody target

Gate 5 will establish this exact chain:

```text
src/common-ship/relief-circuit.ts
        ↓
cartridges/relief-circuit.ship.json
        ↓ registered compilation
src/arcs/relief-circuit.ts
        ↓
cartridges/relief-circuit.arc.json
```

The current `RELIEF_CIRCUIT_CANDIDATE` is a preparation source derived from the complete Book III starter. It fixes identity, destination, control question, and cross-run obligations while preserving the starter's authored mixed crew, embodiment profiles, Watch Engine, ship anatomy, translation stack, six viability tests, eight ship-state tracks, and ten Mission Physics invariants. It remains pre-canon until Gate 5 publishes exact artifacts and acceptance receipts.

## Required crew ecology

The canonical founding population must include materially different profiles whose differences change feasible watches and allocations. At minimum:

- a dry human-scale response body supported by the inherited host baseline;
- a large aquatic care lineage requiring pressure and liquid-medium continuity;
- a large high-gravity maintainer whose public access changes passage and structural design;
- a distributed Manyborn mediator whose personhood crosses carriers and habitat bands;
- a nine-year analyst lineage whose transit and standby consume a politically significant life fraction;
- a distributed Counterborn vessel fork whose body overlaps routing, translation, memory, and ship systems.

Profiles remain conditions of participation rather than occupations. Roles, qualifications, authority, and refusal are authored separately.

## Common Watch law

Every executable watch must be evaluated by Arc's one composition authority and return exact feasibility receipts for:

1. role coverage;
2. temporal overlap;
3. habitat compatibility;
4. translation resilience;
5. handoff continuity;
6. life-fraction fairness.

The player must be able to inspect why a proposed watch is lawful or unlawful, which actor is absent, which body is excluded, which intermediary becomes indispensable, and which single points of failure remain. World may render these receipts; it may not recompute them.

## Ship-state inheritance

The eight engine-owned tracks are:

- Habitat Integrity
- Temporal Coherence
- Translation Trust
- Roster Resilience
- Stores and Care
- Continuity
- Visibility
- Compatibility Debt

Every successful operation must record exact before-and-after values, authored reasons, challenge, outcome, cycle, and any clamp. Partial and failure outcomes must remain playable and consequence-bearing rather than silently resetting the watch.

## Campaign path

Gate 5 must author a complete sequence covering the Book III operational cycle:

```text
ordinary life aboard the ship
→ inspect mission and decision horizon
→ read body, role, clock, environment, and dependency profiles
→ compose or refuse a Common Watch
→ allocate habitat bands, translation paths, direct interfaces, standby, stores, and emergency authority
→ resolve pressure
→ conduct the handoff
→ establish precedent
→ reach the Lamp District
→ conduct one connected relief and descent operation
→ return with changed ship and Tomb state
```

The exact number and names of watches remain a Gate 5 authoring decision. The sequence must contain enough distinct operations that changing the roster, allocation, or precedent produces materially different state and later options.

## Lamp District connection contract

The connected run must preserve both source planes and both state sets. At docking, the portable run must carry:

- the Relief Circuit's exact source, compiled Arc identity, ship state, selected watch, dependencies, and precedents;
- the Lamp District's exact source, compiled Arc identity, Long Alarm, signature, visibility, map, habitat, and constituency state;
- an auditable transfer record naming people, medicine, stores, evidence, translation paths, environmental loads, and exposure consequences;
- the return effects inherited by the ship after the descent.

The connection must not be implemented as a receiver-only scene transition. Arc must own the authored transition or a versioned cartridge-to-cartridge handoff object that both players preserve losslessly.

## Common Ship Forge target

The authoring product must provide guided and exact-source views over the same `common-ship-pocket/1` object. It must support:

- identity, nine pressures, evidence, faction receipts, cast responsibilities, and control question;
- seven ship systems, six temporal dimensions, seven translation layers, and six Common Watch tests;
- embodiment profiles, role requirements, decision horizons, allocations, handoff ledgers, precedents, consequences, state effects, and Mission Physics;
- exact local draft custody;
- registered validation and compilation;
- deterministic authored-founding campaign sweeps;
- source and Arc export, installation, and direct opening in the ordinary player.

## Gate 5 automated acceptance

Gate 5 is complete only when one exact Arc head passes:

- strict source validation and exact source recovery;
- equality among code authority, portable source, and compiled artifact;
- deterministic artifact digest;
- all six composition categories with meaningful rejection fixtures;
- every embodiment profile bound to the exact named founding cast;
- multi-seed full-campaign completion with zero gate bypasses and no engine warnings;
- at least one lawful and one unlawful watch demonstrated from the same candidate roster;
- exact final ship-state and precedent inheritance;
- exact connected Lamp District transfer and return fixture;
- Common Ship Forge edit, persistence, compile, refusal, playtest, install, and export tests;
- complete Arc regression suite and production build.

## Gate 6 dependency

World Gate 6 may begin read-only receiver preparation against the registered Common Ship source plane, but it may not bundle The Relief Circuit, assign a Program number, ship a management surface, or claim browser acceptance until the exact Gate 5 Arc candidate is accepted. World must vendor that exact Arc head and preserve the same resolver, composition, state, save, and custody authority.

## Control question

Can the Relief Circuit reach the Lamp District without making one body, clock, interface, or emergency doctrine the native form through which every passenger must survive?
