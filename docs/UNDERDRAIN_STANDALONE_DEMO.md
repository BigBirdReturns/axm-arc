**Status:** post-v1 demonstration donor  
**Release effect:** none  
**Book IV effect:** none

## Creator idea

A new creator begins with one sentence:

> A municipal plumber is drafted into a war against the hidden fungus kingdom causing every drain problem in town.

The resulting original cartridge is **UNDERDRAIN: The Bloom Below**. It uses no names, characters, visual assets, dialogue, or setting from an existing franchise.

## Long-running ensemble-comedy rails

The package encodes the durable production lessons extracted from the American Dad discussion without copying that series' expression:

1. stable character methods generate plot rather than bending to it;
2. municipal institutions play absurd stakes completely straight;
3. the household B-plot materially collides with the action A-plot;
4. escalation follows from prior choices;
5. the ostensible enemy receives a legible causal model;
6. a terminal result pays persistent state;
7. the episode may restore ordinary function, but cannot erase precedent or debt.

The episode rail is:

```text
backflow at breakfast
  → municipal draft
  → Dax monetizes the panic
  → fungus aquifer cause is revealed
  → Rhea chooses a method
  → Crown Pump action
  → accepted action consequence
  → inherited fungal embassy / audit / rationing pressure
```

## Arc authority

`src/demos/underdrain/` owns:

- one validated engine-1.4 Arc;
- one three-objective `axm-action-profile/1`;
- three player methods;
- one `axm-narrative-rails/1` constitution;
- five stable actor policies;
- the pre-action episode state;
- one `axm-action-narrative-binding/1` with success, partial, and failure consequences.

The action result is never inferred from narrative preference. `ingestAcceptedActionReceipt` first replays and verifies the exact `axm-action-receipt/1`, then emits a fact and consequence candidate through the ordinary narrative sorter and commit authority.

## Standalone World product

The exact authoring manifest is mirrored by the World single-file demo. World may:

- explain the episode;
- collect the player method;
- render the 30 Hz action loop;
- collect keyboard, pointer, and touch input;
- produce a provisional trace and terminal state;
- export a provisional receipt.

World may not:

- turn a provisional result into an accepted action fact;
- alter the accepted outcome;
- close an obligation without Arc;
- assign a campaign effect to physical or browser observations.

## Acceptance

The Arc donor must prove deterministic action compilation, all three methods on the same episode rail, real successful and failed receipt return, and the SHA-256-pinned authoring manifest.

The World donor must prove a genuinely standalone file, cold desktop/mobile entry, inspectable rails, terminal action play, nine deterministic bot runs, provisional authority, and zero external runtime dependency.
