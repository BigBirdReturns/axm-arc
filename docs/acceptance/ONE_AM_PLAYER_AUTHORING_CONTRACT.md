# The 1 AM Player Authoring Contract

The 1 AM player is tired, cold, and unwilling to perform interpretation work on behalf of the product. This is not a request for a simpler story. It is a requirement that the authored experience carry its own identity, motive, action, consequence, and continuation.

The player must be able to answer, from play rather than documentation:

1. Who am I?
2. What am I doing now?
3. Why does it matter?
4. What changed because I acted?
5. What can I do next?

A valid action specification is not yet a valid authored experience. The contract rejects these substitutions:

- an objective label attached to a kill counter;
- a critical reveal printed only after combat;
- a choice remembered only as result-screen copy;
- campaign-state data that never becomes visible or playable;
- a next-episode sentence with no successor scene;
- recovery that returns the player to title or repeats exposition.

Every authored objective must bind a concrete player verb to:

- a real interaction or mechanism;
- a story purpose;
- an observable state change;
- a collision between character methods;
- any critical reveal produced during play.

The deterministic action law may remain stable across routes. Route choice must still change at least one of knowledge, available interactions, NPC behavior, spatial route, or visible world state before the result screen.

`axm-one-am-player-contract/1` is an authoring qualification contract. World must separately prove that the runtime actually presented and fulfilled it.
