# Lineage brief — the squad liberation campaign

**Reference lineage:** the tactical liberation campaign — dispatch squads to
take strongholds across a map, accrete doctrine/alignment as a hidden currency,
chase an ending you sense you're missing.
**Our cartridge:** `cartridges/severed-march.arc.json` (clone #2).

## Core tension
Speed vs. thoroughness. Every stronghold you rush leaves reputation on the
table; every one you clear fully costs you tempo. The campaign never lets you
optimize both, and the choice compounds — early haste narrows your late options.

## Retention loop
A hidden alignment/reputation currency the game never shows you directly. You
finish a run sensing there was a "true" outcome you didn't reach, and you
replay to chase it. The opacity is the engine of replay: legible progress ends
the loop; sensed-but-unseen progress sustains it.

## Progression arc (the beats)
Onboarding skirmishes (learn dispatch and composition) → the mid-campaign wall
where a roster built for brute force meets content that demands specific
composition — the first real investment decision → the long middle of accreting
doctrine → the finale that reads your accumulated alignment and pays off (or
withholds) the ending accordingly. The composition wall is the beat that turns
a roster from a pile of stats into a set of decisions.

## Tuning lessons
- The difficulty ramp must *force* the composition pivot mid-campaign — a curve
  that lets one strategy ride to the end has no beats.
- The finale should be tight enough to feel earned but reachable by a prepared
  party — the "almost lost it" band, not a coinflip and not a formality.
- Alignment feedback is the master knob: too hidden and players never learn the
  system exists; too visible and it stops being a discovery. The whole game
  lives on that dial.

## Known failure modes
- Alignment fully hidden → players never realize there's a system → no replay.
- Alignment fully visible → it becomes a checklist → the mystery dies.
- Finale too generous → the campaign's accumulated stakes cash out as nothing.

## How our cartridge honors it — and where it doesn't yet
The chapters-as-progressionTiers, the Law↔Chaos analog as drama basis +
precedents, and the "Sigils of the March" gate to the finale carry the beats.
The conformance sweep landing **14/15 seeds** clearing the difficulty-78 finale
is almost exactly the "tight but earned" band the lineage wants — this is the
cartridge whose tuning best matches its brief. **Divergence to bank:** the
hidden-alignment retention loop is only lightly expressed (precedents accrete
but their pull toward a gated "true" outcome is subtle); a future tuning pass
could make the alignment currency's finale influence sharper without making it
*legible* — threading exactly the master-knob needle above.
